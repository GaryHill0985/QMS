# SESC-PRO-03 · Management Review

**Clause 9.3 of ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2026 and ISO 45001:2018, and clause 9.1.3 of ISO 9001 —
how the Managing Director reviews the Integrated Management System at planned intervals, on
analysed evidence, and decides what changes.**

> **Status: DRAFT, version 0.1, 26 October 2026. Not approved and not issued.**
> This procedure is issued for review by the Chief Operating Officer and approval by the Managing
> Director. Until the Approval block at Part 9 is completed it evidences nothing. **No management
> review has been minuted since 10 January 2023** (CLAUDE.md §10; SESC-POL-05 A31 ¶171(e)). The
> three signed documents each committed to a minuted review by 30 September 2026; that date has
> passed and the review has not been held. This procedure records that as overdue and does not
> re-date it quietly.
>
> **The Employer holds no ISO certification of any kind and does not claim to.**

---

## Part 1 — Purpose, scope and words used

1. This procedure states when the management review is held, who attends, what is put in front of
   them and in what form, what they must decide, how the decisions are recorded and how they are
   followed. It is the procedure SESC-IMS-00 Part 10 (o) says does not exist, and on approval it
   replaces the three separate review statements at SESC-POL-16 Q23 ¶68, SESC-POL-05 A8 ¶51 and
   SESC-POL-08 O1 ¶6 and E11 ¶64 with one review of one system. The management review is the one
   record that shows leadership operating rather than being written down (SESC-IMS-05 ¶12).

2. **Scope.** The whole Integrated Management System as described in SESC-IMS-00: every clause,
   every process at SESC-IMS-00 ¶19, every site and Unit 19, for all three disciplines together.
   The review is not three reviews on three days; one business is reviewed once.

3. **The sole-director clause.** Where the Employer has only one director, any function reserved to
   the Board which requires independence from a person involved in the matter will be performed by
   the independent professional adviser named in the Approval block. In this procedure that clause
   bites at ¶9.

4. **Words used.** "The review" is the full management review at ¶6(a). "The interim review" is the
   quarterly meeting at ¶6(b). "The pack" is the analysed evidence put before the review under
   Part 3. "The minutes" are the record of the review under Part 5. "An action" is a decision with
   an owner and a date.

## Part 2 — When, and who

### 2.1 Planned intervals

5. The standards require review at planned intervals. For a business of the Employer's size and
   stage, once a year is too seldom to steer by and once a month is a management meeting, not a
   review. The Employer therefore operates:

6. | | What | When | Chaired by | Minimum attendance |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | (a) | **The management review** — the full review of every input at Part 3 and every output at Part 4 | Once a year, in **November**, after the first-cycle audit results and before the objectives for the coming year are set; and additionally within one month of a major nonconformity, an enforcement action, a RIDDOR-reportable injury, a significant environmental incident, or a change in the Employer's ownership, premises, scope or standards editions | Managing Director | Managing Director; Chief Operating Officer; Contracts Manager |
   | (b) | **The interim review** — the status of actions, the measures, incidents and nonconformities for the quarter, and anything that cannot wait for (a) | Quarterly, in **February, May and August**, aligned with the quarterly review of SESC-REG-01 (SESC-POL-16 ¶65) and the quarterly consultation meeting (SESC-IMS-05 ¶38(c)) | Managing Director | Managing Director; Chief Operating Officer; Contracts Manager |
   | (c) | **The monthly report** — open and overdue actions, findings and consultation items, reported to the Managing Director; not a review | Monthly | — | Chief Operating Officer and Contracts Manager to Managing Director (SESC-POL-16 ¶63(c); SESC-POL-05 ¶50; SESC-IMS-05 ¶38(d)) |

7. The dates are entered in SESC-REG-02 for the year ahead at the November review. A review that
   is postponed is re-dated in SESC-REG-02 with the reason, and is not postponed twice.

### 2.2 Attendance

8. The Managing Director chairs and cannot delegate the chair. The Chief Operating Officer presents
   the pack and takes the minutes. The Contracts Manager attends as Quality Representative, Health
   and Safety Officer and Environmental Manager and answers for the operation of the arrangements.
   A Site Supervisor attends the November review to speak for the sites. The retained external
   health and safety adviser is invited to the November review for the health and safety and
   compliance items. Where a workers' representative of employee safety is elected or appointed
   (D26), the representative attends the November review and the interim reviews for the
   consultation items; where there is none, the Health and Safety Officer presents the SESC-FRM-08
   record and what the workforce raised at the quarterly consultation meeting is a standing input.

9. **Independence.** Where an item requires a judgement independent of the Managing Director — the
   adequacy of the resources he has provided, a Speak Up matter, a conflict of interest, his own
   compliance with the system — the independent professional adviser attends for that item and his
   view is minuted (SESC-IMS-00 ¶58). The Chief Operating Officer identifies such items in the pack
   in advance.

## Part 3 — Inputs: the pack (clause 9.3.2; ISO 9001 clause 9.1.3)

### 3.1 The rule

10. **Nothing is reviewed from memory.** Every input is in a written pack issued by the Chief
    Operating Officer to every attendee at least five working days before the review. The pack is
    analysis, not raw data: for each measure it states the figure, the period, the source record,
    the trend against the previous period and against the objective, and what the Chief Operating
    Officer concludes from it. Where a figure cannot be given because the record does not exist,
    the pack says so and names the records-not-yet-held item and its date. **A pack that reports
    "no data" for an item is correct; a pack that omits the item is not.** The pack is the
    Employer's analysis and evaluation under ISO 9001 clause 9.1.3 and is retained with the minutes.

### 3.2 What the pack contains

11. The inputs the three standards require, in the order the review takes them:

    | # | Input | Source record | Owner of the input |

    | 2 | **Changes in context**: external and internal issues (SESC-REG-06), the needs and expectations of interested parties (SESC-REG-05), compliance obligations and other requirements (SESC-REG-09), significant aspects (SESC-REG-10), risks and opportunities (SESC-REG-01, SESC-REG-08), and climate change (SESC-REG-06 CLI-00) | The registers, as changed since the last review | Chief Operating Officer |
    | 3 | **Extent to which the policy and objectives have been met** | SESC-IMS-05 policy; SESC-IMS-06 objectives register | Managing Director's own assessment, from the pack |
    | 4 | **Performance and effectiveness — quality**: customer satisfaction and feedback; complaints; nonconformities and corrective actions; the quality measures at SESC-POL-16 Q23 ¶67; monitoring and measurement results; audit results; performance of external providers; adequacy of resources; effectiveness of actions on risks and opportunities | SESC-FRM-05 log; complaints log; SESC-REG-02; SESC-FRM-04 and the supplier register; audit reports | Quality Representative |
    | 5 | **Performance and effectiveness — environment**: fulfilment of compliance obligations (the SESC-REG-09 evaluation record); environmental nonconformities and incidents; the environmental data set at SESC-POL-08 E15 and SESC-CRP-01; progress on environmental objectives; audit results; communications from interested parties including complaints | SESC-REG-09 evaluation record; SESC-FRM-01 and 02 environmental entries; the data set; audit reports | Environmental Manager |

12. The interim review takes items 1, 4 to 6 in summary form, 8 and 10, and anything the Chief
    Operating Officer or the Contracts Manager cannot leave until November.

### 3.3 Analysis, not figures

13. For the measures at items 4 to 6 the pack shows the current period, the previous period and the
    same period a year earlier once that exists; expresses rates per 100,000 hours worked where the
    measure is normalised, taking hours from the payroll and timesheets (SESC-POL-05 A7 ¶47;
    SESC-IMS-04 ¶12); and states a conclusion: improving, stable, deteriorating, or no data. A rising
    count of near misses and of consultation items raised is a positive indicator (SESC-POL-05 A7
    ¶45; SESC-IMS-05 ¶42) and is presented as one.

## Part 4 — Outputs: what the review decides (clause 9.3.3)

14. The review does not end until the Managing Director has decided, and the minutes record, each
    of the following, even where the decision is "no change":

    (a) **whether the system remains suitable, adequate and effective** — suitable to what the
        Employer does, adequate to the standards and the obligations, and effective in achieving
        the policy and the objectives; and, where it is not, what changes;

    (b) **opportunities for improvement** adopted, each as an action with an owner and a date;

    (c) **changes to the system**: to the scope statement, the policy, the objectives, the
        processes, the documents (each becomes a change under SESC-PRO-01), and to the registers;

    (d) **the objectives for the coming period**, set or revised in SESC-IMS-06, and the resources
        for them;

    (e) **resources** to be provided, with the Managing Director's decision on every item in the
        pack that asked for money, time or people, including a decision not to provide and why;

    (f) **actions where objectives have not been achieved**, and actions arising from the
        inventories of records not yet held whose dates have passed;

    (g) **the integration** of the system into the business — anything the review found to be run
        beside the business rather than as part of it;

    (h) **implications for the strategic direction** of the Employer — a new trade, a new customer
        type, a scheme to join or leave, the certification programme itself; and

    (i) **for health and safety**, the effectiveness of consultation and participation, and what the
        workforce is told about the review's decisions and how.

15. **Decisions reserved to the Managing Director** — spend, contractual terms, statements to a
    customer, insurer or certification body, appointments — are taken by him at the review or are
    minuted as deferred with a date. No one else takes them, and this procedure does not assume
    them.

## Part 5 — The record: minutes and actions

16. The minutes are written by the Chief Operating Officer in the form at Annex B, issued to every
    attendee within five working days, confirmed by the Managing Director in writing or by
    signature, and take the next free SESC-REC-nn reference. They are indexed in
    `portal/records-index.yaml` against clause 9.3 of the three standards and ISO 9001 9.1.3, and
    they are the record the gap report tests for. They record: who attended and in what role; every
    input considered, by reference to the pack; every decision at ¶14(a) to (i); every action with
    owner and date; every item deferred, with the reason and the date; and the independent
    adviser's view where ¶9 applied.

17. The pack is retained with the minutes. Both are retained for five years (SESC-POL-16 Annex B;
    SESC-POL-05 Annex B; SESC-POL-08 Annex B). Minutes that name an individual's conduct, health or
    a Speak Up matter are held in the records store under SESC-POL-14, and the repository copy is
    redacted to the decision.

18. **Actions** are entered on the corrective action log the same day, tracked by the Chief
    Operating Officer, reported monthly under ¶6(c), and are item 1 of the next pack. An action is
    closed only when what it required exists and the closure is verified; it is not closed by the
    passage of time or by being re-minuted.

19. **Telling the workforce.** The decisions that affect site — changes to controls, to the policy,
    to the objectives, to what is monitored, and the answers to what the workforce raised — are
    briefed at the next toolbox talk on every active site and recorded on SESC-FRM-06, and the
    consultation items are closed on SESC-FRM-08 with the feedback date (SESC-IMS-05 ¶40).

## Part 6 — Measuring the review

20. From the first review, and reported in each subsequent pack: reviews held against the dates in
    SESC-REG-02; the proportion of pack items with data, as against "no data"; actions raised and
    the proportion closed by their date; and the number of records-not-yet-held items whose dates
    have passed. **No measure has yet been taken.**
