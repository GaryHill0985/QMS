# SESC-PRO-08 · Fire Door Installation

**Clauses 8.1, 8.5.1, 8.5.2, 8.5.5, 8.6 and 8.7 of ISO 9001:2015, with 7.1.5.1 for the gauges,
and the operational-control part of clause 8.1 of ISO 14001:2026 — how the Employer surveys for,
specifies, installs, checks, releases and hands over fire doors, who may do the work, where the
hold points are, and what record and photograph exists for every door.**

> **Status: DRAFT, version 0.1, 4 November 2026. Not approved and not issued.**
> This procedure is issued for review by the Chief Operating Officer and approval by the Managing
> Director. Until the Approval block at Part 8 is completed it changes nothing, and no fire door
> is installed under it.
>
> **The Employer holds no ISO certification of any kind and does not claim to. The Employer holds
> no third-party certification for fire door installation, from any scheme, and does not claim
> to.** Every installation under this procedure is evidenced on the basis stated at ¶7, and on
> nothing else. **No fire door has yet been installed under this procedure, and none of the
> records it describes exists for any door** (Part 7).
>
> **Scope.** Fire door work is not named in the scope statement at SESC-IMS-04 v0.3 ¶14, which
> removed it under decision D11. SESC-IMS-04 v0.4 names it (decision D28). This procedure is
> drafted alongside v0.4 and depends on the Managing Director signing it.

## Part 1 — Purpose, scope and words used

1. This procedure states how the Employer takes an instruction to install fire doors, surveys
   each opening, selects a doorset whose test evidence covers that opening, installs it in
   accordance with the manufacturer's instructions and BS 8214, measures and tests it, releases
   it, hands it over with the records the client needs, and keeps those records so that any door
   the Employer has installed can be traced from its opening to the evidence behind every
   component in it. It is the procedure that the forms SESC-FRM-09 to SESC-FRM-14 and the
   register SESC-REG-13 operate under, and SESC-WI-02 is the one-page version of it for site.

2. **Scope.** Every fire door, fire doorset and fire door assembly the Employer installs, in any
   building, for any customer. It covers new installation into an existing or new structural
   opening, and the replacement of an existing door. Remedial repair of existing fire doors and
   planned inspection and maintenance follow the same records where they apply, and are given
   their own inspection and repair records in a later revision (Part 7(n)). It applies to all
   Staff of the Employer, including all employees & subcontractors, who survey, order, receive,
   install, check, sign for or hand over a fire door.

3. **The sole-director clause.** Where the Employer has only one director, any function reserved
   to the Board which requires independence from a person involved in the matter will be
   performed by the independent professional adviser named in the Approval block.

4. **Words used.** "The Employer" is SESC Solutions Ltd. A "doorset" is a door leaf, frame,
   seals, ironmongery and any glazing supplied together by one manufacturer as a tested or
   assessed unit. A "door assembly" is a fire door built from components from more than one
   source, where the evidence must show the combination is permitted. The "rating" is the fire
   resistance period and smoke control the client's fire strategy or specification requires,
   stated as FD30, FD30S, FD60 or FD60S, or otherwise as the specification states. "Test
   evidence" is the manufacturer's fire test report, assessment or field-of-application report,
   or certificate, that states what the doorset achieved and the configurations it may be
   installed in. The "field of application" is the range of opening sizes, wall types, leaf
   sizes, ironmongery and glazing the evidence covers. "Gap" is the clearance between the leaf
   and the frame or threshold, measured where ¶26 says. A "hold point" is a step at which the
   work stops until a named person has checked the record and the photograph for that step.
   The "golden thread" is the client's set of information about its building; the handover pack
   at ¶30 is the Employer's contribution to it. The "responsible person for fire door work" is
   the person named at SESC-FRM-14 who authorises operatives and signs completion certificates;
   the title a certification scheme gives that person is taken from the scheme document when it
   is read (Part 7(j)), not assumed here.

5. **What this procedure does not do.** It does not decide what rating a door must have: the
   client's fire risk assessment, fire strategy or specification does, and where none is given
   the Employer asks in writing and does not proceed on an assumption (¶11). It does not set gap
   tolerances or fixing spacings: the doorset manufacturer's data does, and the form carries the
   manufacturer's figure beside the measured one (¶26). It does not state what any scheme,
   standard or regulation requires from memory: a requirement is read off the issuing body's
   own document and recorded with its source (CLAUDE.md §2), and the instruments this procedure
   relies on are indexed at SESC-REG-09 (¶8). It does not carry out or review the client's fire
   risk assessment. It does not claim, state or imply any third-party certification (¶7). It
   does not operate the health and safety controls for the work, which are in the risk
   assessment and method statement under SESC-POL-05 and are briefed under SESC-PRO-06 ¶8.

6. **The basis of every installation, which is also the only wording used for it.** Every
   completion certificate, handover document, quotation, email and conversation that describes
   what the Employer has done uses this statement and no other:

   > *Installed in accordance with BS 8214 and the doorset manufacturer's installation
   > instructions, using components matching the doorset's test evidence, by trained operatives
   > under SESC supervision.*

7. **What is not said.** No document, form, label, certificate, quotation or statement by any
   person working under the Employer's control states or implies that the Employer, the
   installation or the operative is certified, approved, registered or accredited under BM TRADA
   Q-Mark, FIRAS or any other third-party scheme for fire door installation. The Employer intends
   to apply for third-party certification and records that intention at Part 7(j); until a
   certificate is held and read, nothing is claimed. A client who needs a third-party-certified
   installation is told so in writing before the work is quoted (¶10), by the Managing Director,
   because a statement to a customer is reserved to him (CLAUDE.md §12.1).

## Part 3 — What the installation is measured against

9. **The instruments and documents this procedure relies on.** Each is named here for what it
   does in this procedure; what each requires is read from the instrument itself, on the issuing
   body's site, and indexed at SESC-REG-09 with the date read. Where the register does not yet
   carry a row for the fire door trade, Part 7(g) says so.

    (a) **The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005.** Places the duty to keep fire
        precautions, including fire doors, in efficient working order on the responsible person
        for the premises — the client, not the Employer. The Employer's part is to install so
        that the door can perform as its evidence says, and to hand over what the responsible
        person needs to maintain it (¶30). SESC-REG-09 CO-24 carries the Order for the Employer's
        own premises only; the trade row is Part 7(g).

    (b) **The Building Regulations 2010 and Approved Document B.** Fire doors are part of the
        compartmentation and means of escape the building work must achieve. Approved Document B
        is the statutory guidance, and it is the client's fire strategy or designer, not the
        Employer, who determines from it what rating each opening needs. SESC-REG-09 CO-35
        carries the Regulations as they apply to the Employer's building work.

    (c) **The Building Safety Act 2022** and the competence and contractor duties inserted into
        the Building Regulations at Part 2A (SESC-REG-09 CO-36): organisational capability,
        individual competence, co-operation, and the duty to supervise and to provide
        information. SESC-FRM-14 and the handover pack are how this procedure evidences them for
        fire door work. Where the building is a higher-risk building under that Act, the client's
        golden thread requirements are taken from the client in writing before work starts.

    (d) **The Construction Products Regulations** (SESC-REG-09 CO-37). Where the doorset is
        covered by a designated standard, the manufacturer's Declaration of Performance and
        marking are checked and a copy held on the job file (SESC-FRM-10). The Employer
        manufactures nothing and modifies no doorset beyond what the manufacturer's instructions
        permit.

    (e) **BS 8214, the code of practice for fire door assemblies.** The installation practice the
        Employer works to, as the statement at ¶6 says. A copy is to be held by the Employer and
        read before the first installation (Part 7(h)); until then no paragraph of it is quoted.

    (f) **The doorset manufacturer's installation instructions and test evidence.** The document
        that governs every dimension, fixing, seal, gap and component on the door. Where the
        instructions and BS 8214 differ, the manufacturer's instructions apply, because they are
        what the test evidence was produced against, and the difference is recorded on
        SESC-FRM-11.

10. **The client is told the basis before the work is quoted.** Because the Employer is not
    third-party certified, the Managing Director confirms with the client in writing, before the
    instruction is accepted, that installation on the basis at ¶6 is acceptable for that
    instruction. SESC-TPL-05 is the wording. The client's written reply is filed on the job file
    and its reference entered on SESC-FRM-12. **Hold point 1: no survey is carried out and no
    doorset is ordered until that reply is held.**

11. **The rating comes from the client.** The rating required for each opening is taken from the
    client's fire risk assessment, fire strategy, specification or schedule, and its source is
    written on SESC-FRM-09. Where the client gives none, the Contracts Manager asks in writing
    and the Managing Director decides whether to proceed; no rating is assumed, and no door is
    installed to a rating the Employer chose.

## Part 4 — The process, stage by stage

### 4.1 Instruction and job set-up

12. On receipt of the instruction the Contracts Manager opens the job under the Employer's
    normal contract review (SESC-POL-16 Q4 and the readiness review at ¶17(d)), allocates the job
    number, and creates on the job file a `Fire Doors` folder with one subfolder per door,
    named by the door identifier the client uses or, where the client has none, by `D01`, `D02`
    and so on in the order of the client's schedule. Every record and photograph for a door goes
    in that door's subfolder and nowhere else (¶32).

13. The risk assessment and method statement for the job is prepared and approved under
    SESC-POL-05 and briefed on site before work starts (SESC-REG-12 CR-27). It covers the
    removal of the existing door, the handling of the doorset, work at the opening, the
    fire-stopping products and any hot works, and the disposal of the removed door and
    packaging. It is a separate record and this procedure does not repeat it.

### 4.2 Pre-installation survey — SESC-FRM-09, one per door

14. Before any doorset is ordered, an authorised operative or the Contracts Manager surveys every
    opening and records it on SESC-FRM-09: the door identifier and location; the rating required
    and its source (¶11); the structural opening height, width and wall thickness, measured at
    three points each where the opening is out of true; the wall or partition construction and
    its condition; the existing door, frame and ironmongery and their condition; whether the
    Employer removes and disposes of the existing door; the handing and direction of swing; any
    services, ducts or finishes in the reveal; and the access constraints for the work. The
    survey photograph, stage 1 at ¶32, is taken of the existing door in place.

15. **Hold point 2.** The Contracts Manager reviews each survey and records on it one of three
    outcomes: the opening is suitable and the doorset may be selected; the opening needs
    builder's work first, which is specified and priced before the doorset is ordered; or the
    opening cannot take a doorset whose evidence covers it, which is reported to the client in
    writing by the Managing Director. No doorset is ordered against a survey without this review.

### 4.3 Product selection and evidence — SESC-FRM-10, one per door

16. The doorset is selected so that the opening recorded on SESC-FRM-09 falls within the field
    of application of the doorset's test evidence: the opening size and wall type, the leaf size
    and configuration, the glazing, and every item of ironmongery. The Employer prefers a
    complete doorset from one manufacturer. Where components are sourced separately the
    evidence must show that the combination is permitted, and the Contracts Manager records on
    SESC-FRM-10 which document shows it.

17. The doorset and every component are bought from an approved source under SESC-PRO-05 and
    SESC-POL-17: the manufacturer, a distributor it authorises, or a merchant holding a trade
    account in the Employer's name. On receipt the goods are checked against the order and the
    evidence, the marking and documentation are checked for the signs of counterfeit or
    non-conforming product at SESC-POL-17 and SESC-POL-19, and any doubt stops the installation
    until the manufacturer has answered in writing. Nothing is substituted on site. A hinge, a
    closer, a seal or an intumescent product that is not the one the evidence names is not fitted,
    whatever is in the van.

18. SESC-FRM-10 is completed before anything is fitted. For the doorset as a whole it records the
    manufacturer, the product reference, the type and reference of the test evidence, whether a
    copy is held on the job file, and the field-of-application check against the survey. For
    each of the leaf, the frame, the intumescent seals, the smoke seals, the hinges with their
    number, the closer, the latch or lock, the glazing and the glazing system, the signage and
    the threshold or drop seal, it records the product fitted, the evidence reference that
    covers it, and whether it matches the tested or assessed configuration. **Hold point 3: the
    Contracts Manager approves SESC-FRM-10 with every row answered, and only then does
    installation start.** A row answered "does not match" stops the job for that door, and the
    door is not installed until the row is corrected by a change of component or by written
    confirmation from the manufacturer that the evidence covers it.

### 4.4 Who may do the work

19. Fire door work is carried out only by operatives authorised for the task on SESC-FRM-14 and
    supervised as ¶8 says. The competence each task needs is at SESC-REG-12 CR-45 to CR-48, the
    evidence that a named person holds it is on SESC-FRM-03 and the Training Matrix in the
    records store (SESC-PRO-06 Part 6), and SESC-FRM-14 is the record that the responsible
    person for fire door work has looked at that evidence and authorised that person for those
    tasks. An operative who is not yet authorised works alongside one who is, does not sign
    SESC-FRM-11 or SESC-FRM-12, and is recorded on SESC-FRM-11 as present. The first
    authorisations do not yet exist (Part 7(d)).

### 4.5 Installation — SESC-FRM-11, one per door

20. The existing door, frame and ironmongery are removed as the RAMS says, and the structural
    opening is cleared, checked against the survey and photographed empty (stage 2). Any
    difference from the survey is written on SESC-FRM-11 and, where it takes the opening outside
    the field of application, the work stops and ¶15 applies again.

21. The removed door, frame and packaging are construction waste under the Employer's duty of
    care (SESC-POL-08 ¶34; SESC-REG-09 CO-43) and are segregated, carried and disposed of as
    SESC-POL-08 says, with the transfer note reference recorded on SESC-FRM-11. Where the client
    retains the removed door, that is recorded instead. This is the operational control claimed
    under ISO 14001 clause 8.1, and the aspect is to be added to SESC-REG-10 (Part 7(i)).

22. The frame is fixed with the fixing type, number, spacing and edge distance the manufacturer's
    instructions state, into the substrate the evidence covers, plumb, square and true, and
    packed at every fixing with the packing the instructions permit. The fixing type and the
    spacing used are written on SESC-FRM-11 beside the manufacturer's figure. **Hold point 4:
    before any fire-stopping is applied, the Site Supervisor checks the frame fixing and packing
    against the instructions, photographs it (stage 3) and signs SESC-FRM-11 for that step.**
    A frame that is fire-stopped before this check is recorded as a nonconformity (¶35).

23. The gap between the frame and the structural opening is fire-stopped with the product the
    manufacturer's instructions name or permit for that gap width and substrate, applied as
    that product's own instructions state, and the product name and the evidence reference that
    covers its use in that position are written on SESC-FRM-11. The completed fire-stopping is
    photographed before architraves or finishes cover it (stage 4). A product chosen because it
    was to hand, rather than because the evidence covers it, is a nonconformity.

24. The intumescent seals and, where the rating requires it, the smoke seals are fitted in the
    positions, grooves and lengths the evidence shows, continuous and uncut except where the
    instructions say; the hinges are fitted in the number, position and with the screws the
    evidence states; and the seals and hinges are photographed (stage 5). The closer, the latch or
    lock, any other ironmongery, the glazing and the signage are fitted as the evidence covers
    them and photographed (stage 6). Intumescent protection for hinges, locks and closers is
    fitted where the evidence calls for it, and its absence where it calls for it is a fail.

### 4.6 Gaps, gauges and functional tests

25. **The gauges.** Gaps are measured with a feeler gauge or gap gauge identified by a number,
    checked against a steel rule before first use on each job, and recorded by that number on
    SESC-FRM-11. A gauge that is worn, bent or unmarked is not used. This is the Employer's
    arrangement under ISO 9001 clause 7.1.5.1 for this work; no calibration certificate is
    claimed for any gauge, and SESC-POL-16 Annex D governs the instruments the Employer
    calibrates. The first gauge check record does not yet exist (Part 7(l)).

26. **The measurements.** With the door closed and latched, the gap is measured at ten points and
    each value written in millimetres on SESC-FRM-11: the hinge side at the top, middle and
    bottom; the lock side at the top, middle and bottom; the head at the hinge end, the middle
    and the lock end; and the threshold gap or undercut. Beside each group the form carries the
    tolerance the manufacturer's instructions state for that doorset, copied from the
    instructions by the operative on the day with the page reference, and each measurement is
    marked pass or fail against that figure. **No tolerance is printed on the form, and none is
    stated in this procedure, because the figure belongs to the doorset.** Where the
    instructions give no figure for a position, the operative records "not stated" and the
    Contracts Manager obtains it from the manufacturer before release. The gauge in the gap is
    photographed at one position on each side and at the threshold (stage 7).

27. **The functional tests**, each recorded pass or fail on SESC-FRM-11: the door self-closes
    fully from the fully open position and from a position approximately 5° from closed, and
    latches without assistance; it does not bind on the frame, the floor or the seals through
    its full swing; the closer is adjusted to the manufacturer's instructions and holds the leaf
    against the stop; and the signage required by the rating is fitted to both faces where the
    evidence or the specification requires. The finished door is photographed on both faces
    (stage 8), and the doorset label, the certification marks and the component markings are
    photographed legibly (stage 9).

28. **Hold point 5.** The Site Supervisor witnesses the measurements and the tests, checks that
    every photograph at ¶32 is held in the door's subfolder, and countersigns SESC-FRM-11. Any
    fail is corrected, re-measured or re-tested and recorded on the same form as a second entry,
    never by overwriting the first (D15). A fail that cannot be corrected by adjustment within
    the manufacturer's instructions is a nonconformity (¶35) and the door is not released.

### 4.7 Release and the completion certificate — SESC-FRM-12

29. A door is released to the client only when SESC-FRM-09, SESC-FRM-10 and SESC-FRM-11 are
    complete for it with every hold point signed, every gap and test passed, and the nine
    photographs held. The installer then completes SESC-FRM-12 for the door: the job number,
    client, site and building; the door identifier, location and rating; the doorset
    manufacturer and the test evidence reference; the references of the three records; the
    installation date; the statement at ¶6, word for word; the installer's name, signature and
    date; and the supervisor's name, signature and date. The Contracts Manager signs as
    supervisor. Where the client wants one certificate for the job, SESC-FRM-12 is completed
    once with every door listed, and the three per-door records are referenced for each. The
    certificate carries the note at ¶7 that no third-party certification is stated or implied.
    This is the release record under ISO 9001 clause 8.6.

### 4.8 Handover — SESC-FRM-13, one per job

30. On completion of the job the Contracts Manager compiles the handover pack and records it on
    SESC-FRM-13: the schedule of doors with identifier, location, rating, manufacturer, evidence
    reference and completion certificate reference; a copy of each SESC-FRM-12; the
    manufacturer's installation and maintenance instructions and the Declaration of Performance
    where there is one; the photograph set for each door; the recommended inspection interval,
    with the document it was read from, and the list of what an inspection checks; and the
    Employer's contact for maintenance, remedial work and questions. The pack is given to the
    client's responsible person, who signs SESC-FRM-13 for receipt, and a copy is kept on the job
    file. This is the post-delivery activity under ISO 9001 clause 8.5.5, and the handover is the
    natural point at which planned inspection and maintenance is offered.

31. The recommended inspection interval is not a figure fixed by this procedure. It is read from
    the manufacturer's maintenance instructions and from the guidance the client's fire risk
    assessment relies on, written on SESC-FRM-13 with its source, and the next inspection due
    date is calculated from it and entered on SESC-REG-13.

### 4.9 Photographs — the standard

32. **Nine photographs per door are required, at these stages, and a door with fewer is not
    released.** They are the evidence that the hold points were held, and on a door whose
    fire-stopping is covered by an architrave they are the only evidence.

    | Stage | What the photograph shows | Form and hold point |
    |---|---|---|
    | S01 | The existing door in place, before removal, both faces where accessible | SESC-FRM-09 |
    | S02 | The empty structural opening, cleared, before the frame goes in | SESC-FRM-11 |
    | S03 | The frame fixed and packed, before any fire-stopping | SESC-FRM-11, hold point 4 |
    | S04 | The fire-stopping complete around the frame, before architraves or finishes | SESC-FRM-11 |
    | S05 | The intumescent and smoke seals and the hinges fitted | SESC-FRM-11 |
    | S06 | The ironmongery and the closer fitted | SESC-FRM-11 |
    | S07 | The gauge in the gap, at one position on each side and at the threshold | SESC-FRM-11, hold point 5 |
    | S08 | The finished door, both faces | SESC-FRM-11, hold point 5 |
    | S09 | The doorset label, certification marks and component markings, legible | SESC-FRM-11, hold point 5 |

33. **Naming and storage.** Each file is named `JOBNO_DOORID_STAGE_YYYYMMDD.jpg`, for example
    `JN1234_D01_S03_20261110.jpg`, where the stage is the code in the table. A second photograph
    at the same stage adds `-2`. Each is stored in the job folder in the `Fire Doors/<Door ID>/`
    subfolder (¶12) on the day it is taken, and nowhere else. A photograph that stays on a phone
    is not held. Photographs show the work and not people; where a person is unavoidably in
    shot, SESC-POL-14 applies. Until the SESC Platform takes photographs (D18, D20), they are
    filed by hand by the operative and checked by the Site Supervisor at hold point 5.

### 4.10 The register — SESC-REG-13

34. Every door installed is entered as one row in SESC-REG-13 by the Contracts Manager within
    five working days of release: the job, site and building; the door identifier and location;
    the rating; the manufacturer and the evidence reference; the installation date and the
    installer; the gap result; the completion certificate reference; and the next inspection due
    date. The row is the identification and traceability record under ISO 9001 clause 8.5.2, and
    the register is the asset list from which planned inspection and maintenance is offered. The
    repository holds the register's columns, issued empty; the working register with rows is
    held in the records store, and the rows move to the SESC Platform without re-keying (D20),
    which is why the columns are fixed here and not in a spreadsheet.

## Part 5 — When something does not match

35. **Stop, record, do not improvise.** A component that is not the one the evidence names; an
    opening or substrate outside the field of application; a frame fire-stopped before hold point
    4; a gap outside the manufacturer's figure that adjustment within the instructions cannot
    bring in; a functional test that fails after adjustment; a photograph missing at release; or
    an instruction from anyone to proceed regardless — each is a nonconformity, is raised on
    SESC-FRM-05 under SESC-PRO-04, and stops release of that door. The door is not released by
    fitting what is to hand, by widening a tolerance, or by leaving a stage unphotographed. This
    is the control of nonconforming output under ISO 9001 clause 8.7.

36. **The client is told by the Managing Director.** Where a nonconformity changes what the
    client receives, when, or on what basis, the Managing Director tells the client in writing.
    No operative, supervisor or manager makes that statement (CLAUDE.md §12.1). What the client
    is told is filed on the job file with the SESC-FRM-05 reference.

37. **A door that has been installed and is later found not to match its record** is treated as
    a nonconformity on the day it is found, whoever finds it, and the client is told. The
    Employer does not wait for the next inspection.
