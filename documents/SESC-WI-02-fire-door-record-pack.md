# SESC-WI-02 · Fire Door Record Pack

*Which form is used when, by whom, and what has to exist before the next step starts. This is
SESC-PRO-08 on two pages. Where the two differ, the procedure governs.*

> **Status: DRAFT, version 0.1, 4 November 2026. Not approved, not issued, not posted.** The
> forms it points at are drafts. Nothing is to be filled in on site until SESC-PRO-08 is approved.

---

## Page 1 — Which form, when

1. **One pack per job. One set of per-door records per door.** Every form carries the job
   number and the door identifier, and every photograph is named with both.

2. **The forms, in the order they are used:**

   | Step | Form | One per | Who fills it in | Before the next step |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 0 | **SESC-TPL-05** Client confirmation of the installation basis | Job | The Managing Director sends it; the Contracts Manager files the reply | **Hold point 1.** No survey, no order, until the client's reply is on the job file. |
   | 1 | **SESC-FRM-09** Pre-Installation Survey | Door | An authorised operative or the Contracts Manager | **Hold point 2.** The Contracts Manager reviews it and writes the outcome before any doorset is ordered. Photo S01. |
   | 2 | **SESC-FRM-10** Product Evidence Record | Door | The Contracts Manager, from the manufacturer's documents | **Hold point 3.** Every row answered, every component matches, approved — then installation starts. A "No" stops the door. |
   | — | **SESC-FRM-14** Operative Authorisation Record | Person | The responsible person for fire door work | Nobody does a fire door task they are not authorised for on this form. Held in the office, not on site. |
   | 3 | **SESC-FRM-11** Installation Checklist and Gap Record | Door | The installer; the Site Supervisor signs the hold points | **Hold point 4** — frame fixed and packed, photo S03, signed before any fire-stopping. **Hold point 5** — ten gaps against the manufacturer's tolerance, the functional tests, photos S07 to S09, signed before release. Photos S02 to S09. |
   | 4 | **SESC-FRM-12** Installation Completion Certificate | Door, or job | The installer signs; the Contracts Manager signs as supervisor | Only when FRM-09, FRM-10 and FRM-11 are complete, every hold point is signed, every gap and test passed, nine photographs held. |
   | 5 | **SESC-FRM-13** Handover and Maintenance Schedule | Job | The Contracts Manager; the client signs for receipt | The pack is complete before it is handed over. The next inspection date goes on the register. |
   | 6 | **SESC-REG-13** Fire Door Asset and Install Register | One row per door | The Contracts Manager, within five working days of release | From the FRM-12. A door with no certificate has no row. |
   | Any time | **SESC-FRM-05** Nonconformity and Corrective Action | Event | Anyone who finds it | A component that does not match, a gap that will not come in, a missing photo, a frame fire-stopped before hold point 4: stop, raise it, do not improvise. |

3. **The nine photographs per door.** Named `JOBNO_DOORID_Sxx_YYYYMMDD.jpg` and filed in
   `Fire Doors/<Door ID>/` on the job file the same day. A photo on a phone is not held.

   | Code | What it shows | On which form |
   |---|---|---|
   | S01 | Existing door in place, before removal | FRM-09 |
   | S02 | Empty structural opening | FRM-11 |
   | S03 | Frame fixed and packed, **before fire-stopping** | FRM-11, hold point 4 |
   | S04 | Fire-stopping complete, before architraves | FRM-11 |
   | S05 | Seals and hinges fitted | FRM-11 |
   | S06 | Ironmongery and closer fitted | FRM-11 |
   | S07 | Gauge in the gap, each side and threshold | FRM-11, hold point 5 |
   | S08 | Finished door, both faces | FRM-11, hold point 5 |
   | S09 | Door labels and certification marks, legible | FRM-11, hold point 5 |

4. **The one thing every document says about what we did**, and nothing else:
   *Installed in accordance with BS 8214 and the doorset manufacturer's installation
   instructions, using components matching the doorset's test evidence, by trained operatives
   under SESC supervision.* We are not third-party certified for fire doors, and no form,
   label, email or conversation says or implies that we are.

## Page 2 — The rules that stop a door

5. **The tolerance is the manufacturer's, not ours.** Copy it from the installation instructions
   onto FRM-11, with the page, before you measure. The form has no figure printed on it on
   purpose.

6. **Nothing is substituted.** If the hinge, closer, seal, intumescent or fire-stopping product
   in the van is not the one the evidence names, it is not fitted. Stop and call the Contracts
   Manager.

7. **The frame is not fire-stopped until the Site Supervisor has signed hold point 4** and photo
   S03 is in the folder. Fire-stopping first and asking after is a nonconformity.

8. **A fail is re-recorded, never overwritten.** Correct it, measure again, and fill in a new
   FRM-11 entry that gives the reference of the first one. Nobody edits a record.

9. **The client is told by the Managing Director.** If something changes what the client gets,
   when, or on what basis, you tell the Contracts Manager and the Managing Director tells the
   client. Nobody else.

10. **Stop work you cannot do safely or correctly.** That authority sits above everything on
    this sheet (SESC-IMS-04 ¶13).

## Control

11. **Purpose.** This instruction tells fire door operatives and Site Supervisors which record
    is made at which step of SESC-PRO-08, and what must exist before the next step. The forms
    and their fields are defined in SESC-FRM-09 to SESC-FRM-14; the register in SESC-REG-13;
    the photograph standard at SESC-PRO-08 ¶32 and ¶33.

12. **Clauses.** ISO 9001 7.5.3: the records are made once, referenced, dated and never
    overwritten. ISO 9001 8.5.2: every record and photograph is identified to its job and its
    door. No other clause is cited; the controls themselves are SESC-PRO-08's.
