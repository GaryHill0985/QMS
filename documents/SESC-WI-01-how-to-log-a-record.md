# How to log a record on site

*Page 1 is the site sheet. The form links and QR codes go into the table at paragraph 2 once
the forms are built (`build/workspace_forms/README.md` §7). Until then this document is a
draft and must not be posted.*

## Page 1: the site sheet

1. **If it happened, or nearly happened, log it the same day, from your phone.** It takes two
   minutes. Nobody is disciplined for reporting. A near miss that nobody reports is the one that
   happens next week.

2. **Pick the form:**

   | What happened | Form | Sign-in |
   |---|---|---|
   | Something **nearly** went wrong, or you have spotted a hazard | FRM-01 Near miss | None. No name needed. |
   | Someone was **hurt**, something was damaged, or there was a dangerous occurrence | FRM-02 Accident | None |
   | You gave a **toolbox talk** | FRM-06 Toolbox talk | SESC account |
   | You **inspected** the site | FRM-07 Site inspection | SESC account |
   | Work or a product **did not meet a requirement** | FRM-05 Nonconformity | SESC account |

   *[QR code and short link for each form go here.]*

3. **Serious injury, or anyone taken to hospital:** make the area safe, get first aid, and
   **phone the Health and Safety Officer straight away**, then fill in FRM-02. The form does
   not replace the phone call. The Managing Director decides whether it is reported under
   RIDDOR and makes the report.

4. **Photos:** take them. The forms cannot accept photos yet. Send them to the Health and
   Safety Officer and say which report they belong to.

5. **Got something wrong?** You cannot edit a report after you submit it, and nobody else can
   either. Submit the form again, and in the last question say what it corrects. That is how
   the Employer keeps records that an auditor can trust.

6. **Write what you saw, not who did it.** Keep people's health details and other personal
   information out of the free-text boxes unless the form asks for them.

## Control

7. **Purpose.** This instruction tells employees & subcontractors how to create the records
   that the Employer's integrated management system depends on. The forms and their fields are
   defined in SESC-FRM-01, 02, 05, 06 and 07. How they are built and held is set out in
   `build/workspace_forms/README.md`. Records are held in a restricted Google Workspace Shared
   Drive under decision D15, and never in this repository.

8. **Clauses.** ISO 45001 10.2: this is how incidents and near misses reach the people who act
   on them. ISO 45001 7.4.2: it tells workers how to communicate them. Clause 7.5.3 of all
   three standards: records are captured once, dated, referenced and never overwritten. This
   instruction does not by itself satisfy ISO 45001 5.4 worker participation, and it is not
   cited against that clause.

9. **Training and competence records (SESC-FRM-03) and supplier evaluations (SESC-FRM-04)**
   are office records. They are not listed on the site sheet.
