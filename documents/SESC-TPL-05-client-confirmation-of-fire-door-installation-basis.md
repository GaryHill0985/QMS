# SESC-TPL-05 · Fire Door Installation Basis Confirmation

**The email the Managing Director sends a client before a fire door instruction is accepted,
asking it to confirm in writing that competent installation to BS 8214 and the manufacturer's
instructions, without third-party scheme certification, is acceptable for that instruction.
Hold point 1 of SESC-PRO-08.**

> **Status: DRAFT, version 0.1, 4 November 2026. Not approved and not issued.** Until
> SESC-PRO-08 is approved this template is not used.
>
> **Who sends it.** A statement to a customer about the basis on which the Employer will carry
> out work is reserved to the Managing Director (CLAUDE.md §12.1). The Contracts Manager may
> prepare the email from this template; the Managing Director sends it from his own account, and
> the client's reply is filed on the job file (SESC-PRO-08 ¶10).

---

## Part 1 — When it is used, and why

1. The Employer holds no third-party certification for fire door installation. Some clients,
   specifications and frameworks require one; many accept competent installation evidenced by
   records. Which applies to a given instruction is the client's decision, and the Employer asks
   before it surveys or orders anything, so that no door is installed on a basis the client
   later finds it cannot accept. The reply is the first record on the job file for the fire
   door work and its reference is entered on every SESC-FRM-12 completion certificate.

2. The email says what the Employer will do, in the one wording used everywhere (SESC-PRO-08
   ¶6), says plainly what the Employer does not hold (¶7), and asks one question. It does not
   argue that certification is unnecessary, does not describe any scheme, and does not say the
   Employer intends to apply for one, because an intention is not a fact the client can rely on.

3. **Fill in** the fields in square brackets from the instruction. **Do not change** the two
   paragraphs in bold. Send to the person who placed the instruction and copy the client's
   responsible person for fire safety where known.

## Part 2 — The email

4. Subject line: *[Job number] — fire door installation at [site]: basis of installation,
   confirmation requested*

5. Body:

   > Dear [name],
   >
   > Thank you for your instruction [client reference] for the installation of [number] fire
   > doors at [site, building], received on [date].
   >
   > Before we survey the openings or order doorsets, we would like to confirm with you in
   > writing the basis on which we will carry out the work.
   >
   > **Each door will be installed in accordance with BS 8214 and the doorset manufacturer's
   > installation instructions, using components matching the doorset's test evidence, by
   > trained operatives under SESC supervision. For every door you will receive a
   > pre-installation survey, a product evidence record matching each component to the
   > doorset's test evidence, an installation checklist with the measured gaps against the
   > manufacturer's tolerance and the functional tests, nine stage photographs including the
   > fire-stopping before it is covered, a completion certificate and a handover and
   > maintenance schedule.**
   >
   > **SESC Solutions Ltd does not hold third-party certification for fire door installation
   > under BM TRADA Q-Mark, FIRAS or any other scheme, and the completion certificate will say
   > so. The installation will be evidenced by the records above and not by a scheme
   > certificate.**
   >
   > Could you please confirm by reply that installation on this basis is acceptable for this
   > instruction, or let us know if your specification or your fire risk assessment requires a
   > third-party-certified installer, in which case we will say so before any work proceeds.
   >
   > The rating required for each opening will be taken from [the client's fire risk
   > assessment / fire strategy / specification, reference]. If any opening's rating is not
   > stated there, we will ask you before ordering.
   >
   > Yours sincerely,
   >
   > Steven A Coffen
   > Managing Director, SESC Solutions Ltd
   > 01747 445 509
