# SESC-IMS-00 · Integrated Management System Manual

**The top document of the Integrated Management System for ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2026 and
ISO 45001:2018 — what the system is, how it is put together, and where each requirement is met.**

> **Status: DRAFT, version 0.1, 6 October 2026. Not approved and not issued.**
> This manual is issued for review by the Chief Operating Officer, for comment by the Employer's
> retained external health and safety adviser, and for approval by the Managing Director. Until
> the Approval block at Part 11 is completed it evidences nothing.
>
> **The Employer holds no ISO certification of any kind and does not claim to.** No certification
> body has ever assessed the Employer. Every statement in this manual is written to be true on the
> day it is read, or is marked at Part 10 as a record that does not yet exist, with the date by
> which it will.

## Part 1 — Purpose, and how to read this manual

1. This manual describes the Employer's single Integrated Management System and shows, clause by
   clause, where each requirement of ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2026 and ISO 45001:2018 is met. It
   is the first document a reader, an auditor or a customer should open. It does not repeat the
   content of the documents it points to; it says briefly how the Employer meets each requirement
   and names the document, register or form that holds the detail.

2. None of the three standards requires a manual. The Employer keeps one because a reader who
   opens the system for the first time needs a map, and because the Employer's documents are
   held as structured source in a version-controlled repository rather than as a folder of files,
   so without a map the structure is not obvious.

3. **How to read it.** Parts 3 to 9 follow the clause structure common to the three standards:
   context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation and improvement. Each
   part states how the requirement is met, points to the governing document, and, where the
   governing document or the record behind it does not yet exist, says so. Part 10 gathers every
   such absence into one dated inventory. Annex A gives the clause map this manual claims for
   itself. Annex B is the document map, which lists each clause of each standard against the
   document that addresses it, and is the page an auditor will use most.

4. **Standards and editions.** The Employer builds to ISO 9001:2015 with Amendment 1:2024, written
   to be ready for ISO 9001:2026, which was published in September 2026; which edition the
   Employer certifies to will be settled with the certification body. The Employer builds to
   ISO 14001:2026, the 2015 edition having been withdrawn on 15 April 2026. **The Employer does
   not yet hold a copy of ISO 14001:2026 and nobody working on this system has read it; every
   ISO 14001 clause reference in this manual and in the system is carried from the withdrawn
   edition and is marked unverified until the current edition is bought and read** (Part 10).
   The Employer builds to ISO 45001:2018 with Amendment 1:2024.

5. **Words used.** "The Employer" is SESC Solutions Ltd. "The system" is the Integrated
   Management System described here. "The repository" is the version-controlled store described at
   Part 6, section 6.5. "Workspace" is the Google Workspace tenancy that holds records as a
   stopgap under Part 6, section 6.5. The Employer is working towards being *certified*;
   certification bodies are *accredited*. The phrase "ISO accredited" is not used of the Employer
   anywhere in the system.

6. Where this manual and a document it points to disagree, the pointed-to document governs, and
   the disagreement is raised as a nonconformity against this manual.

## Part 5 — Planning (clause 6)

### 5.1 Actions to address risks and opportunities (clause 6.1)

28. **Business risks.** The Employer's risks are scored, owned and dated in **SESC-REG-01
    Business Risk Register**, version 1.0, signed by the Managing Director on 12 August 2026. The
    issues from which many of them arise are in SESC-REG-06, with opportunities held in a
    separate field so that the two are never merged.

29. **Hazard identification and assessment of OH&S risks** (ISO 45001 clauses 6.1.2.1 and
    6.1.2.2) are carried out job by job through the risk assessments and method statements
    required by SESC-POL-05, and for the premises through the arrangements in the same document.
    The methodology, the hierarchy of control and the review triggers are stated there. The
    records are the assessments themselves, held with each job. **No fire risk assessment exists
    for the Employer's own premises at Unit 19**; that is a statutory duty under the Regulatory
    Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 before it is a requirement of ISO 45001, and it is at Part 10,
    item (e).

30. **Environmental aspects and impacts** (ISO 14001 clause 6.1.2). **The Employer does not have
    an environmental aspects and impacts register of its own.** The register previously held was
    another organisation's and has been withdrawn from use. SESC-POL-08 describes the approach;
    the register itself, built from the Employer's own activities, does not exist. **This is the
    defining ISO 14001 document and the highest-priority gap in the system.** It is at Part 10,
    item (f), and it cannot be written until ISO 14001:2026 has been bought and read.

31. **Compliance obligations and legal requirements** (ISO 14001 clause 6.1.3; ISO 45001 clause
    6.1.3). The Employer's compliance obligations register is dated 2020 and is not current. A
    current register, and an evaluation of compliance against it, are at Part 10, item (g).
    SESC-REG-02 Assurance Calendar, version 1.0, signed 17 August 2026, schedules the recurring
    obligations the Employer has identified, with owners and frequencies, and is the operative
    document for those obligations in the meantime.

32. **Planning action.** Actions arising from the registers above are owned and dated within the
    register that raises them, or are logged as corrective actions under Part 9 once the
    nonconformity and corrective action log exists (Part 10, item (i)).

### 5.2 Objectives and planning to achieve them (clause 6.2)

33. **Quality objectives** are set for the year commencing 1 September 2026, within SESC-POL-16.
    No earlier dated set exists. **Environmental and occupational health and safety objectives

### 5.3 Planning of changes (ISO 9001 clause 6.3)

34. Changes to the system are planned through the controlled-document process at Part 6,
    section 6.5: a change is proposed as a visible difference against the current version,
    reviewed by a named person and approved by the Managing Director before it is issued, and the
    review triggers in each document's control block force a review when the circumstances the
    document depends on change. Changes to the business — a new trade, a new site, a new
    scheme — are review triggers for SESC-IMS-04 and for this manual.

### 6.5 Documented information (clause 7.5)

39. **What the system consists of.** The system's documented information is held in four tiers:

    | Tier | What | Series | Where it lives |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | 1 | This manual | SESC-IMS-00 | The repository |
    | 2 | The clause spine SESC-IMS-04 to SESC-IMS-10, and the policies | SESC-IMS-nn, SESC-POL-nn, SESC-CRP-nn | The repository (SESC-IMS-04 and later); the signed policies as branded PDFs pending migration into the repository |
    | 3 | Procedures, work instructions, capability sheets and form templates | SESC-PRO-nn, SESC-WI-nn, SESC-CAP-nn, SESC-FRM-nn, SESC-TPL-nn | The repository |
    | 4 | Registers of things, and records of what happened | SESC-REG-nn, SESC-REC-nn | Registers in the repository as data; records in Workspace as a stopgap, moving to the SESC Platform |

40. **Document references.** Every document carries a reference in the series above. The number
    is never reused and an issued document is never renumbered. In the SESC-IMS series the number
    is the clause the document covers, 04 to 10; **this manual takes 00 as the overview that sits
    ahead of the clause chapters.** The full list of references in use is **SESC-REG-07 Document
    Register**.

41. **Control block.** Every controlled document carries a six-field control block — reference,
    title, version, status, issue date and review date — together with its owner, its approver and
    the clauses it claims. For every document held in the repository these fields are written
    once, in the document's front matter, and the cover, the control table, the running header
    and the footer are **generated** from it; nobody types a version number into a table. The
    footer of every controlled document reads *uncontrolled when printed*. Documents for defence
    establishments carry OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE markings on every page.

42. **Creating and changing a document.** Every change to a document in the repository is made as
    a proposed change showing the exact difference against the current version, is reviewed by a
    named person, and is recorded with its author, date and reason by a cryptographically signed
    commit. The main line of the repository cannot be changed except through that route, and its
    history cannot be rewritten. **A change to a controlled document is authored and reviewed by
    the Chief Operating Officer and approved by the Managing Director. Two named humans. No
    automated tool approves, signs or merges a change to a controlled document.** An automated
    check runs on every proposed change and fails it if the control block is malformed, if a
    clause is cited that does not exist in the standard, if the review date has passed, if house
    style is broken, or if a file containing personal data has been placed in the repository.

43. **Signed documents are amended, never regenerated.** A document is edited in place with its
    revision history intact. The twenty-one signed documents listed in Annex B exist today as
    branded PDFs outside the repository; they are controlled by SESC-REG-07 and will be migrated
    into the repository one at a time, the first as a reference implementation, without changing
    their content.

44. **Documents of external origin** — the standards, legislation, customer specifications,
    scheme requirements and manufacturers' instructions — are identified in the document that
    relies on them and controlled by that reference. The Employer's own licensed copies of the
    standards are at Part 10, item (a).

45. **Records.** A record is created at the point of work by the person who did the work, on the
    form designed for it. Records are **append-only**: a record is never overwritten, and a
    correction is a new record that references the original. Every record is attached to a job, a
    person, an organisation or an asset. Records containing personal data are held in Workspace
    under SESC-POL-14 and SESC-POL-13 and never in the repository; the repository holds only
    record metadata, so that the twelve-month staleness test at ¶48 can be computed. Retention
    periods are those stated in the policy that governs each record type and, for design
    records, the seven years in SESC-POL-16 Annex B. **Record capture in Workspace is designed and built in the repository and
    is not yet live; no live record exists** (Part 10, item (n)). The record capture section of
    the SESC Platform replaces the Workspace stopgap as soon as it is built.

46. **A documented procedure for document and record control does not yet exist.** The rules in
    this section are the operative statement until SESC-PRO-01 Control of Documented Information
    is issued (Part 10, item (o)).

## Part 7 — Operation (clause 8)

### 7.1 Operational planning and control (clause 8.1)

47. Every job is planned before it starts. The planning record is the quotation, which states what
    is and is not offered; the risk assessments and method statements required by SESC-POL-05,
    which carry the environmental controls required by SESC-POL-08; and, for the design
    activities named in the scope statement, the design records at section 7.3. Where the
    Employer is Principal Contractor, the construction phase plan is the planning record for the
    project. Work that the Employer subcontracts remains under the Employer's control through
    section 7.4. **The hierarchy of control** (ISO 45001 clause 8.1.2) is applied through the risk
    assessments; **management of change** (clause 8.1.3) is addressed through the same job
    planning when the work changes, and through ¶34 when the system changes.

48. **Requirements for products and services** (ISO 9001 clause 8.2) are determined at enquiry
    and confirmed in the quotation and the customer's order; changes are agreed in writing before
    the work changes. **The design carve-out at SESC-IMS-04 ¶58 has not yet been applied to the
    standard quotation**, which until it is amended excludes all design while the Employer carries
    out the design named in the scope statement. That is a known inconsistency, recorded at
    Part 10, item (p), and nothing is issued to a customer that contradicts it once it is approved.

### 7.2 Supplier and subcontractor evaluation and control (clause 8.4)

49. Suppliers and subcontractors are selected, evaluated and monitored under **SESC-POL-12
    Supplier Code of Conduct**, with **SESC-POL-17 Counterfeit Materiel Policy and Control Process**
    governing the provenance of materials and **SESC-POL-10 Modern Slavery, Labour Standards and
    Human Rights Policy** and **SESC-POL-18 Financial Sanctions and Trade Compliance Policy**
    governing who the Employer will trade with. **SESC-FRM-04 Supplier and Subcontractor
    Evaluation** (draft) is the evaluation record. **An approved supplier and subcontractor
    register of the Employer's own, with evaluation records against it, does not yet exist**
    (Part 10, item (q)). A certificate presented by a supplier is accepted only after it has been
    verified with the issuing body.

### 7.3 Design and development (ISO 9001 clause 8.3)

50. Clause 8.3 applies to heat loss calculation, emitter and heat pump sizing, MCS solar PV
    design, and the specification of ventilation for mould and damp remediation, and to nothing
    else. The determination, its evidence and the professional indemnity position are at
    SESC-IMS-04 Part 7. Design inputs, controls, outputs and changes for those activities are the
    calculations and specifications produced under the certification schemes concerned and are
    retained for seven years under SESC-POL-16 Annex B. **The record that the Employer's
    professional indemnity cover extends to ventilation specification is not held**, and nothing
    customer-facing says that it is until it is (SESC-IMS-04 ¶58(d); Part 10, item (b)).

### 7.4 Production and service provision, release and nonconforming outputs (ISO 9001 clauses 8.5 to 8.7)

51. Work is carried out under controlled conditions: the method statement, competent people
    (section 6.2), suitable equipment (section 6.1), inspection as the work proceeds, and test and
    commissioning records at completion. **SESC-FRM-07 Site Inspection** (draft) is the record of
    an integrated walk-round covering safety, environment and workmanship. Property belonging to
    customers — occupied buildings, their contents and their occupants' data — is identified and
    protected through the job planning at ¶47 and, for personal data, under SESC-POL-14. Release to the customer is by handover with the
    test certificates and operation and maintenance information the job requires. **Nonconforming
    outputs** are controlled through **SESC-FRM-05 Nonconformity and Corrective Action** (draft)
    under Part 9. **SESC-POL-19 Product Integrity, Product Safety and Conformity Policy** governs
    the conformity of what is installed.

### 7.5 Hazard identification, accidents, incidents and OH&S controls

52. Hazard identification is at section 5.1. **Accidents and incidents** are reported, recorded
    and investigated under SESC-POL-05, with **SESC-REG-03 Accident, Incident and RIDDOR
    Register** as the register and **SESC-FRM-02 Accident and Incident Report** (draft) as the
    report form; near misses and hazards are reported on **SESC-FRM-01 Near Miss and Hazard
    Report** (draft), which is anonymous by design. Accident detail naming people is held in
    Workspace under SESC-POL-14. Health and wellbeing arrangements are in **SESC-POL-07 Mental
    Health and Wellbeing Policy** and **SESC-POL-06 Drug and Alcohol Policy**.

### 7.6 Environmental operational control and incidents (ISO 14001 clause 8.1)

53. Operational controls for the Employer's environmental aspects are described in SESC-POL-08,
    with waste arrangements also in SESC-POL-19 Annex A, and are carried into each job's method
    statement. **Because the aspects register does not exist (section 5.1),
    the Employer cannot yet show that every significant aspect has a control**, and does not claim
    to. Environmental incidents are reported on SESC-FRM-01 or SESC-FRM-02 as the case requires
    and corrected under Part 9. **SESC-CRP-01 Carbon Reduction Plan** holds the Employer's
    measured Scope 1 emissions for the 2023 baseline and 2025 reporting years; Scope 2 data for
    Unit 19 is not yet held (SESC-IMS-04 Part 8(j)). The waste carrier registration tier is
    unconfirmed (Part 10, item (r)).

### 7.7 Emergency preparedness and response (ISO 14001 clause 8.2; ISO 45001 clause 8.2)

54. Site emergency arrangements, including fire, are planned job by job through the risk
    assessments and method statements under SESC-POL-05. Business continuity, including loss of
    premises, systems and key people, is planned and tested under **SESC-POL-15 Business
    Continuity Plan**, with the 2026 exercise recorded at **SESC-REC-03**. **For the Employer's own
    premises, no fire risk assessment exists and no emergency plan derived from one** (Part 10,
    item (e)); environmental emergency arrangements for the premises and for site spillage are
    described in SESC-POL-08 and **no record of a test or drill is held** (Part 10, item (s)).

## Part 8 — Performance evaluation (clause 9)

### 8.1 Monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation (clause 9.1)

55. What the Employer monitors, how, when and by whom is scheduled in **SESC-REG-02 Assurance
    Calendar**, which is operated by the Chief Operating Officer and reported to the management
    review. Site performance is monitored through SESC-FRM-07 (draft). Hours worked from the
    payroll and timesheets are the denominator for every normalised measure (SESC-IMS-04 ¶12).

56. **Evaluation of compliance** (ISO 14001 clause 9.1.2; ISO 45001 clause 9.1.2) **has not been
    carried out** against a current obligations register, because none exists (Part 10,
    item (g)). **Customer satisfaction** (ISO 9001 clause 9.1.2) **is not monitored**; it is at
    Part 10, item (t). The analysis and evaluation of results is the business of the management
    review, which has not been held (section 8.3).

### 8.2 Internal audit (clause 9.2)

57. **No internal audit has ever been carried out under any system, for any period, and no audit
    programme exists.** The Employer does not claim otherwise. The programme, when established,
    will cover every clause of the three standards and every process at ¶19 within each
    certification cycle, will be risk-based, and will use auditors who do not audit their own
    work; where the person who wrote or built part of the system also audits, another auditor
    takes that part. **No internal auditor has been appointed and no SESC employee is trained as
    one.** A proposal for an external internal auditor is before the Managing Director. The
    programme, the auditor and the first audit are at Part 10, item (u).

### 8.3 Management review (clause 9.3)

58. The Managing Director reviews the system at planned intervals, at least annually, with the
    Chief Operating Officer and the Contracts Manager present and the independent professional
    adviser attending where a matter requires independence from the Managing Director. The inputs
    are those required by the three standards — the status of previous actions; changes in
    context, obligations and interested parties; the objectives; performance including incidents,
    nonconformities, audit results, compliance evaluation, consultation outcomes, external provider
    performance and customer satisfaction; adequacy of resources; risks and opportunities; and
    opportunities for improvement — and the outputs are decisions and actions, minuted and owned.
    **No management review has been minuted since 10 January 2023.** The next is at Part 10,
    item (h), and it is the first record this manual's own clause map depends on.

## Part 9 — Improvement (clause 10)

### 9.1 Nonconformity and corrective action (clause 10.2)

59. A nonconformity — including an incident, a complaint, a defect, an audit finding and a breach
    of a compliance obligation — is reacted to, recorded, investigated for cause, corrected, and
    the correction checked for effect, on **SESC-FRM-05 Nonconformity and Corrective Action**
    (draft), with workers and their representatives involved where it concerns OH&S. The log of
    those forms is the Employer's nonconformity and corrective action log. **No such log of the
    Employer's own has yet been opened** (Part 10, item (i)). Changes to the system arising from a
    corrective action go through Part 6, section 6.5.

### 9.2 Continual improvement (clause 10.3)

60. The Employer improves the system through the outputs of the management review, the audit
    programme, the consultation mechanism and the corrective action log. **Evidence of continual
    improvement — the record ISO 45001 clause 10.3 e) asks for — does not yet exist, because the
    processes that produce it have not yet run.** The Employer's measure of improvement is that
    the inventory at Part 10 gets shorter, and an item leaves it only when the record it
    describes exists.
